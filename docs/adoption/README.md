# ZONES Open Adoption Guide

## Purpose

ZONES is public and open to use. Use it as a reference model for civil-time
boundary analysis, solar-time scoring, contiguous zone-plan evaluation, or a
bounded state, province, country, or county-level adaptation.

Public use does not create a legal time-zone proposal, regulatory filing,
statutory recommendation, agency endorsement, public referendum position, or
implementation mandate.

## Fast Paths

| If You Are | Start With | What You Can Do |
|---|---|---|
| Public reader | [`README.md`](../../README.md) | Understand the civic-boundary and solar-time scoring model. |
| Researcher | [`research/RESEARCH.md`](../../research/RESEARCH.md) | Inspect what is known, unknown, and publishable. |
| Boundary-data contributor | [`docs/specs/SPEC_INDEX.md`](../specs/SPEC_INDEX.md) | Work from source, geometry, assignment, and review boundaries. |
| Civic or policy reviewer | [`local-adaptation-worksheet.md`](local-adaptation-worksheet.md) | Scope a bounded civil-time question without overclaiming. |

## First Local Adaptation

1. Pick a bounded geography: state, province, country, county set, or current
   legal boundary question.
2. Name the metric question: solar-time error, contiguity, boundary cuts,
   current-law fit, or population-weighted exposure.
3. List source surfaces: legal time assignment, administrative boundaries,
   representative points, population, adjacency, and current-law references.
4. Mark every claim as source-backed, heuristic, held, source-needed, or
   confidence-limited.
5. Produce a short readout: current-law baseline, candidate comparison,
   evidence holds, and next source asks.

## Contribution Targets

- legal source inventories for current time-zone assignments;
- boundary, adjacency, representative-point, or population-source corrections;
- map-caption and publication-scope review;
- civil-time policy, local-government, transport, school, health, commerce, or
  public-language review;
- safer wording that prevents a score fixture from becoming a statutory
  recommendation.

## Gate

Decision: **open_for_reference_review_and_adaptation**

Rationale: ZONES can be reused as an inspectable civic-boundary analysis
pattern. Reuse alone does not create legal, regulatory, statutory, endorsement,
referendum, public-readiness, or implementation claims.
